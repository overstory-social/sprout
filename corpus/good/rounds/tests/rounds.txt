# The watchman hears `:departed` when Marta leaves the yard, and follows
# her out through its one exit: the yard's own `leaves`, read by Ines who
# stays, and the shed's `arrives`, read by Marta who went ahead. The crow
# makes the same crossing on the yard's own tick, naming the shed by its
# own bare identifier rather than a message's bound parameter.
@arrive Marta
@arrive Ines
Marta> go in
  Ines (notice): Marta leaves.
  Ines (notice): A watchman leaves.
  Marta (notice): A watchman arrives.
  Marta (described): There is nothing special about a shed.
@tick
  Ines (notice): A crow leaves.
  Marta (notice): A crow arrives.
