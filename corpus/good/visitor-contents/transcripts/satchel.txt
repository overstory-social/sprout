# Every visitor's first arrival makes them with a satchel of their own, as a
# spawn of their kind would be made, and nothing is said of it. A returning
# visitor comes back with what they carried away, and is never given
# another: Marta keeps hers across a visit, and once she has dropped it in
# the yard she comes back empty-handed.
@arrive Marta
  Marta (described): There is nothing special about a yard.
Marta> inventory
  Marta (said): You are carrying a satchel.
@arrive Ines
  Marta (notice): Ines arrives.
  Ines (described): There is nothing special about a yard.
Ines> inventory
  Ines (said): You are carrying a satchel.
@leave Marta
  Marta (notice): You leave, and take what you carry with you.
  Ines (notice): Marta leaves.
@arrive Marta
  Ines (notice): Marta arrives.
  Marta (described): There is nothing special about a yard.

  Marta (said): You are carrying a satchel.

  Ines (told): Marta puts a satchel down.
@leave Marta
  Marta (notice): You leave, and take what you carry with you.
  Ines (notice): Marta leaves.
