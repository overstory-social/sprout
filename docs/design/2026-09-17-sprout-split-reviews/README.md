# Outside reviews of the Sprout split proposal (2026-09-17)

Six critiques of revision 1 of `../2026-09-17-sprout-split.md`, one
perspective each, produced by Opus-class reviewers reading the proposal
beside the code and the design documents. They are kept verbatim as the
record behind §11 of the proposal, which says what was accepted and
what was declined and why. They describe revision 1; section numbers
and some names (`unit`, `realm`, six packages) refer to that text.

- `review-ops.md` — supportability and operations
- `review-lang.md` — language design correctness
- `review-scale.md` — scalability, concurrency and storage
- `review-priorart.md` — prior art and "just use system X"
- `review-dx.md` — developer experience and adoption
- `review-security.md` — security, abuse and moderation
