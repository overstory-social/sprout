# Playing on the Playdate

The Playdate has no keyboard, so you do not type a command. You build it,
one choice at a time, by turning the crank. This chapter is for the person
holding the console. The app reaches a Playdate the way any game made
outside the catalogue does: as a `.pdx` folder, zipped and sideloaded from
your account at play.date, or sent over USB from the Playdate Simulator's
Device menu. Building that folder is the maintainer's job and is in the
repository's README.

## The shelf

When the app opens you see the shelf: the worlds on your Playdate, one a
line. Turn the crank, or press up and down, to move, and press **A** to open
one. A world shown in grey cannot be opened; the line at the bottom of the
screen says why (it may need a newer app than yours, or be too big for the
Playdate's memory).

## Choosing a name

Before you go in you choose who you are there. The crank (or up and down)
turns through a list of plain names, such as Alder, Birch and Heron. Names
another person in that world already has, and names the world will not
allow, are left out. Press **A** to arrive. If you have been here before, the
name you used last is the first one offered. **B** takes you back to the
shelf.

## Reading

The world's words scroll up the screen as you play, newest at the bottom.
The top line shows where you are, who is with you and which ways lead out.
Turn the crank to read back over what was said; press **A** when you want to
do something.

## The crank sentence builder

Pressing **A** starts a sentence. The bottom of the screen shows what you
have so far, and a choice between `<` and `>`. Turn the crank to change the
choice, and press **A** to take it. The sentence goes in the same order each
time:

1. **What to do**: `take`, `ask`, `go`, and the rest of what the world has
   taught you here.
2. **What to do it to**, once for each thing the action needs: a thing in the
   room, a thing you carry, or a way out.
3. **A word or a number**, when the action needs one: `ask a guard about`
   `weather`. A number is on the crank too; left and right move it by ten.
4. **Done**: press **A** once more to do it.

**B** steps back one choice, and out of the sentence when you are at the
start. If you are building an action that takes several things at once, after
the first the list offers the others, and "that's all" ends the list.

A choice shown in grey cannot be done right now. Its reason is written under
it, such as "The door is locked", so you do not have to try it to find out.

Try it in `Chip Tree`: arrive, press **A**, turn to `ask`, **A**, `a guard`,
**A**, `weather`, **A**, and **A** again to do it.

## Pictures

Some worlds show a picture in a room or when something happens. It appears
above the words, with a caption under it, and stays until a newer picture
replaces it.

## Saves and time

Your place in a world is saved for you after every move. You never save, and
there is nothing to lose by closing the app, switching the Playdate off or
letting its battery run down. Choose **leave world** in the system menu (the
menu button) to go back to the shelf.

The world goes on while you read. Every ten seconds a moment passes, so a
tap that drips, a creature that wanders, or a guard who leaves his post does
so while the app is open. When you are away the world does not run, and when
you come back it catches up quietly: you arrive where you were, with no
account of what happened in between. If the Playdate's clock is set back, the
world never runs backwards.

If the Playdate has no room left for a save, the app says so, stops, and
greys the world on the shelf until a save works again, since a move that is
not kept would be lost anyway. If a save cannot be read at all, the app sets
it aside and starts the world again, and tells you.

## More worlds

The last line on the shelf is `more worlds...`. Press **A** to ask the
Playdate to fetch a list of worlds over Wi-Fi. The first time, the Playdate
asks whether the app may use the network; say yes. Pick a world with the
crank and press **A** to download it. The list is signed, so the app refuses
one that has been changed on the way, and each world is checked before it
joins your shelf. If there is no Wi-Fi set up, or the server cannot be
reached, the screen says so, **B** goes back, and your shelf is as it was.
Worlds you download stay on the shelf, and so do their saves.
