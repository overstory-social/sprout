Newsleak Serif Bold and Roobert 11 Medium are Panic's Playdate fonts, from the SDK's
`Resources/Fonts`, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Roobert 11 Medium is also `pdxinfo`'s `menuMessageFont`: the system draws the network permission
dialog's purpose and the right side of options menu items in it, since the console's own copy of the
face has no comma.
