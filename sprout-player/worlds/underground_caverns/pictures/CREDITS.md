# Picture credits

Every plate in this folder is a 400x240 1-bit composition. The settings (the rooms, the walls,
the furniture) were painted for this world; the figures in the troll, the thief, the cyclops
and the maze, and the sack, the bottle and the dagger, are sprites from the packs below,
placed in those settings. Only CC0 assets are used; the licences were read on the download
page and, where the archive carries one, in its LICENSE file. "Used" lists the plate(s) the
asset is in.
